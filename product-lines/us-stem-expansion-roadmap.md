# US STEM Expansion Roadmap - 2026-27

## Rule for “what students study now”

August-September pacing differs by state, district, and course. Never label one district map as nationwide pacing. Build grade/course-specific editions and use official current sources from multiple jurisdictions. NGSS middle-school expectations are grade-band expectations, not a national grade-by-grade order.

## Priority order after Chemistry

1. Biology - Cells as Systems, membrane transport/osmosis, and data analysis
2. Physics - Kinematics Graphing Boot Camp
3. Math - grade/course-specific number system and algebra foundations
4. Time-sensitive side track - revised AP Statistics 2026-27

## Subject adaptations of the 14-format chassis

### Mathematics

- Center products on worked examples, error analysis, multiple representations, modeling, and spiral retrieval.
- Separate grade/course editions; do not make a vague grades 6-8 packet.
- Every answer key should show reasoning or a verification path, not only final answers.
- Verify generated numeric variants independently and reject accidental fractions/decimals beyond target difficulty.

### Physics

- Pair conceptual reasoning with calculations, diagrams, graphs, units, and experimental data.
- Include misconception repair, especially slope/area interpretation and scalar/vector distinctions.
- AP editions need original representation, experiment-design, and argumentation tasks; not only equation substitution.

### Biology

- Use model revision, authentic-looking but original data, diagram interpretation, case studies, sequencing, and CER.
- Check labels, scale, direction of matter/energy/information flow, and scientifically important exceptions.
- Do not reuse copyrighted journal figures or NC educational datasets in paid products.

### Chemistry

- Use particulate models, measurement, calculation, evidence, SDS awareness, and safe laboratory decisions.
- Safety statements defer to teacher/site procedure and current SDS information.

## 10-format minimum per first family

For each first family, select at least ten of the fourteen standard formats. The recommended first ten are:

1. Core analysis worksheet
2. Task cards
3. Stations
4. Visual guided notes
5. Digital self-check
6. Sort/matching
7. No-lock challenge
8. Picture reveal/maze
9. Reading/case/CER sub plan
10. Quiz A/B + retake

Add lab/performance task, project, scavenger/gallery walk, and spiral review where the subject benefits.
