# Lab Safety Product Line - 14 Formats

## Product strategy

One topic becomes a family of genuinely different instructional uses, not the same questions in different decoration. Target: grades 9-11 general and honors chemistry, beginning-of-year and substitute-teacher use.

| Order | Format | Main use | Required differentiator |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scenario Analysis Worksheet | Teach safe decisions | Core + honors reasoning; full key |
| 2 | Task Cards | Practice/review | Print + digital; three levels; recording sheet |
| 3 | Stations Circuit | Collaborative lesson | Station signs, rotation map, timed options |
| 4 | Visual Guided Notes | Direct instruction | Filled, scaffolded, blank, and slides |
| 5 | Digital Self-Checking | Homework/remote | Forms/Sheets-ready and immediate feedback |
| 6 | Sorting/Matching/Domino Game | Vocabulary and decisions | Self-correcting sequence and small-group mode |
| 7 | No-Lock Escape Challenge | Engagement/review | Logic-based codes; no special locks |
| 8 | Picture Reveal/Color-by-Code | Independent review | Answers drive image; B&W printer-safe option |
| 9 | Reading + CER | Sub plan/literacy | Chunked text, annotations, and text evidence |
| 10 | Quiz/Test System | Summative check | Forms A/B, retake, exit ticket, keys, item analysis |
| 11 | Lab Practical | Performance assessment | Observable skills rubric and remediation |
| 12 | Project/Choice Board | Transfer/creation | Rubric and clear exemplars without copying |
| 13 | Scavenger Hunt/Gallery Walk | Movement review | Classroom route plus seated alternative |
| 14 | Spiral Review | Long-term retention | Mixed cumulative retrieval, not a duplicate worksheet |

Bonus/freebie candidate: editable lab-safety contract and family acknowledgment. It must state that district policy controls and that the resource is not legal advice.

## Shared vetted concept bank

- PPE and clothing
- Chemical labels and SDS awareness
- Heating and glassware
- Housekeeping and aisle safety
- Waste and broken-glass disposal
- Emergency communication and response
- Smelling chemicals by wafting only when instructed
- No unauthorized experiments, food, or drink
- Reporting spills, injuries, and damaged equipment immediately

The shared bank ensures consistency, but each format must use distinct prompts and cognitive work.

## Production and sales sequence

1. Produce formats 1-4 individually and collect teacher-use feedback.
2. Create a mini bundle only after all four pass content, rights, key, and visual gates.
3. Continue formats 5-10 based on observed interest and misconceptions.
4. A full-topic bundle may launch after at least 10 complete formats pass QA.
5. Formats 11-14 add performance, project, movement, and spiral-use value rather than page-count inflation.

Suggested price hypotheses to test, not promises: focused singles $3-$6; fuller stations/lab/escape/digital products $5-$9; four-product mini bundle $11-$18; complete 10-14-format family initially $29-$49. Review competitor position, product depth, and buyer feedback before each listing.

## Production gates

1. **Scope gate:** clear grade, class type, time, prerequisite, and standards fit.
2. **Rights gate:** source ledger complete; no noncommercial or no-derivatives material adapted.
3. **Content gate:** original prompts; chemistry-teacher fact check; no ambiguous correct answers.
4. **Instruction gate:** directions tested without teacher explanation; differentiation is functional.
5. **Answer gate:** every scorable item appears in the key; alternate reasonable answers noted.
6. **Visual gate:** no crude equipment sketches; B&W legible; no clipping/overlap; consistent hierarchy.
7. **Print gate:** US Letter, safe margins, economical ink, student and teacher files separated.
8. **Listing gate:** cover, preview, accurate page count, contents, grade, time, formats, and limitations.
