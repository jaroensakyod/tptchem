Chemistry Math & Measurement Unit | Slides, Practice, Tests & Answer Keys

## Description

Teach the measurement and calculation habits students need before asking them to complete a chemistry worksheet independently. This complete unit follows a coherent sequence: engage, explicit instruction, worked modeling, guided practice, independent practice, an exit ticket, cumulative review, and assessment.

Five lessons develop complete SI-style measurement records, scientific notation, significant figures, dimensional analysis, density, percent error, accuracy, precision, and evidence-based error analysis. Students keep units visible, explain their choices, and diagnose common mistakes instead of only producing calculator answers.
