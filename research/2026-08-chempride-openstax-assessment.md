# Chem Pride and OpenStax Assessment

**Accessed:** 2026-08-11  
**Decision:** Use Chem Pride as the permanent base benchmark for chemistry topic mapping, classroom workflow, and package architecture only. Keep OpenStax Chemistry 2e out of direct generative-AI authoring until reuse permission is confirmed.

## 1. Rights and source status

### Chem Pride / Longwood Flipped Chemistry Classroom

Inspected pages show a public classroom site with Chemistry Units, Course Documents, and Laboratory Activities. The Unit 1 page exposes a consolidated notes packet, practice packet, Regents practice, answer key, and links to video/interactive practice. The Unit 1 and Safety page exposes a safety lab DOCX, a safety map DOCX, a measurement lab PDF, and an aluminum-foil lab template.

No Creative Commons, commercial-use, or adaptation permission was visible on the inspected pages. Treat the site and its downloadable files as **benchmark_only** unless the owner gives written permission.

Do not reuse:

- exact questions, answer choices, explanations, or wording;
- cover art, cartoons, logos, screenshots, or diagrams;
- distinctive page layouts, tables, or packet sequencing closely enough to imitate the source;
- the site's Regents-specific claims as nationwide pacing evidence.

### OpenStax Chemistry 2e

The local reference PDF is the OpenStax Chemistry 2e textbook. Its copyright page identifies a CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license. OpenStax's current Chemistry 2e preface also describes the book as noncommercial, requires ShareAlike for derivatives, directs commercial entities to request permission, and states that the book may not be used to train or be ingested into large language models or generative-AI offerings without permission.

Rights decision for this project: **no commercial adaptation and no direct generative-AI source ingestion without written permission**. The file may remain recorded as a blocked/reference lead, but it must not be used to generate new paid-product prose, questions, answer keys, figures, or close paraphrases.

The existing repository files `chemistry2e-full.txt` and `chem-ch13-15.txt` appear to be extracted textbook text. They are not release assets, and they must not be used in further authoring until the permission question is resolved.

## 2. What we can borrow from the Chem Pride model

The useful transferable pattern is a classroom workflow, not the source's content:

1. Unit landing page or teacher overview
2. Compact notes/reference tool
3. Practice packet with varied tasks
4. Separate answer key
5. Hands-on or scenario activity
6. Optional video/interactive extension
7. Formative check and repair loop

For the CurioNest product family, this maps to:

| Chem Pride pattern | Original CurioNest implementation |
|---|---|
| Unit overview | Teacher guide with targets, prerequisites, time, and use cases |
| Notes packet | Original guided notes and student tool |
| Practice packet | Original scenario, task-card, station, or data tasks |
| Answer key | Full rationale, acceptable alternatives, and misconception notes |
| Lab/activity | Site-policy-aware safety decisions or performance task |
| Video/interactive extension | Optional self-check or digital companion only when separately rights-cleared |

## 3. Curriculum and product implications

Chem Pride's Unit 1 is labeled Math and Measurement and is tied to Regents Chemistry. It is a useful exemplar for beginning-of-course organization, but it is not sufficient evidence for a national General/Honors Chemistry sequence.

OpenStax Chemistry 2e Chapter 1 is useful as a human-readable scope benchmark for chemistry in context, classification of matter, physical and chemical properties, measurements, uncertainty, accuracy, precision, and mathematical treatment. The project should instead verify the sellable sequence through current official district/state sources and rights-cleared references.

For the Lab Safety line:

- keep Scenario Analysis as the first format;
- use the Chem Pride-style combination of student packet, teacher support, and activity as a packaging hypothesis;
- make Task Cards a genuinely new decision task, not a reformatted copy of Scenario Analysis;
- use only rights-cleared free-source photographs or verified symbols with complete attribution;
- do not use code-drawn or ImageGen visuals in this product; if a task needs visual evidence and no suitable rights-cleared image is available, keep the item or product `BLOCKED` rather than making it text-only;
- keep district policy, teacher direction, and current SDS above generic resource language.
