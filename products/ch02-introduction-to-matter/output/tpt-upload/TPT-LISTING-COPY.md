Introduction to Matter Chemistry Unit | Slides, Practice, Tests & Answer Keys

## Description

Teach students how to classify matter from evidence before asking them to complete independent worksheets or a unit test. This complete unit follows a coherent sequence: engage, explicit instruction, worked modeling, guided practice, independent practice, an exit ticket, cumulative review, and assessment.

Five lessons connect observable evidence with particle models, composition, physical and chemical properties, physical and chemical changes, and mixture-separation methods. Students explain why a uniform sample is not automatically pure, evaluate common signs of chemical change, and link each separation method to the physical-property difference it uses.
