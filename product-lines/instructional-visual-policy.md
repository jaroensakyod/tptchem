# Instructional Visual Policy

This policy applies to every new worksheet, task card set, station, assessment, and chemistry product in this repository.

## Non-negotiable rule

A product cannot be called complete, release-ready, certified, or sellable while a required instructional visual is missing.

Visuals are required when students must inspect or distinguish observable evidence, including:

- hazards and safe/unsafe laboratory setups;
- equipment identity, condition, controls, or orientation;
- PPE placement and use;
- labels, GHS symbols, measurement scales, graphs, tables, models, particles, or spatial relationships;
- before/after comparisons and any prompt that asks what students notice in a scene.

Text-only is acceptable for a specific item only when the complete cognitive task is verbal, numerical, procedural, or reflective and no visual evidence is part of the intended reasoning. Every such decision must be justified in the Visual Plan.

## Asset rule

- Use only verified free-source imagery that permits commercial reuse under Public Domain, CC0, CC BY, or another documented compatible license.
- Prefer international, publicly accessible repositories with stable provenance pages, such as Wikimedia Commons and Bioicons/Servier; do not source instructional images from a local worksheet site.
- Record title, creator, direct source URL, license URL/terms, downloaded file, and every placement.
- Do not copy images from Chem Pride or OpenStax unless the exact license permits this commercial use.
- Do not use AI-generated or code-drawn replacements for required instructional images under the current product policy.
- Decorative graphics do not count toward instructional visual coverage.

## U.S. market rule

Unless a product brief explicitly names another market, student-facing TPT resources use American English, US Letter, U.S. grade bands, and Name/Date/Class Period fields. CurioNest is the rights holder/brand only, never an instructional content reference. Buyer-facing files use `© 2026 CurioNest · For classroom use only`.

## Required records

Every product must contain:

1. An item/page-level Visual Plan in its product brief.
2. `required`, `completed`, and `missing` visual counts in source/catalog metadata.
3. A rights ledger and `assets-manifest.json` record for every used image.
4. A QA result that verifies the planned item IDs against actual placements.
5. Full-size, thumbnail, and B&W render inspection.

## Stop rule

If any required visual is missing, unlicensed, unreadable, decorative-only, mismatched between student and teacher versions, or substituted with prohibited generated artwork, the visual gate is `BLOCKED` and the product cannot proceed to certification or the next format.
