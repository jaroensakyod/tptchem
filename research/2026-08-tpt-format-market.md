# TPT Format and Exemplar Research - August 2026

## Method caveat

Teachers Pay Teachers does not expose unit sales. Visible price, ratings, review counts, included files, and recurring product patterns are demand proxies, not proof of future sales. Prices below were observed on 2026-08-11 and may change.

## Market patterns

- Lab Safety Stations ($4.80, 23 pages) combines instructions, student packet, station signs, quiz, and key.
- Lab Safety/Tools/Measurement Task Cards ($4.50; 4.83/5 from 12 visible reviews) includes 72 cards, print + Google Forms, three levels, recording sheet, and key.
- Lab Safety Color-by-Number ($2.99; 4.86/5 from 22 visible reviews) shows that a short resource can sell when its job is precise and the key is complete.
- A four-format lab-safety bundle ($8.50 sale/$13.50 listed; 4.81/5 from 47 reviews) combines visual notes, color-by-number, domino, and comic project.
- Biology escape-room, physics curriculum/lab, and Algebra digital/color bundles show strong demand proxies for reusable families, differentiated versions, print + digital files, rubrics, and complete teacher support.

Use the product chassis in `product-lines/lab-safety-14-format-roadmap.md`. Do not copy competitor language, questions, visual identity, or protected format names. In particular, use **Visual Guided Notes**, not the protected term “Doodle Notes,” unless permission exists.
